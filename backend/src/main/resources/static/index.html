Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ExamHub?
ExamHub is a comprehensive University Exam Authentication & Management System utilizing dynamic QR verification, biometric checks, smart admit cards, and role-based workflows to streamline and secure assessments.
How does QR verification work?
Approved students receive a digital admit card embedded with an encrypted, time-bound QR code. On exam day, supervisors scan this code to instantly verify the student's eligibility in real-time, completely blocking forged physical cards.
Is biometric verification available?
Yes, the backend architecture integrates robust facial and optical framework capabilities to provide a strict secondary layer of authentication prior to exam entry.
Can one university manage multiple colleges?
Absolutely. The built-in Super Admin dashboard explicitly allows a central university entity to onboard, map out, and monitor dozens of affiliated colleges and their respective departments seamlessly.
Is data secure?
ExamHub protects sensitive demographic and verification data with up-to-date transmission encryptions, JWT-bound endpoints, and rigidly enforced Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
Can reports be exported?
Yes, administrators and supervisors can instantly filter data parameters to generate and export audit logs, attendance sheets, and anomaly reports for compliance.
How do students download admit cards?
Once an empowered supervisor approves a student's registration, the admit card status flips to active. The student simply logs into their personalized dashboard and utilizes the direct download prompt.
Can supervisors verify registrations?
Yes. Supervisors and localized admins possess dedicated permission sets within their portal, allowing them to rapidly filter, review, and either authenticate or drop incoming registration bursts.
