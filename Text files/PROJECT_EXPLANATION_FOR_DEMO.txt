WHAT IS OUR PROJECT? (Say this first)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Our project is an "Advanced Examination Authentication System."

In simple words:
    - When a student comes to give an exam, the system verifies that
      the RIGHT student is sitting in the RIGHT seat.
    - It prevents cheating, impersonation, and fraud during exams.
    - Everything is managed digitally — no paper-based attendance,
      no manual verification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT PROBLEM DOES IT SOLVE?
------------------------------------------------------------------------

CURRENT PROBLEM (without our system):
    - Supervisors manually check ID cards — slow and error-prone.
    - Students can send someone else to give the exam (impersonation).
    - No digital record of who attended and who didn't.
    - Fraud cases have no proper tracking system.
    - No automated way to manage exams across multiple colleges.

OUR SOLUTION:
    - Each student gets a unique AES-256 encrypted QR code (Admit Card).
    - Exam Supervisor scans the QR → system instantly fetches student details.
    - Attendance is recorded digitally and automatically.
    - Full role-based system: Super Admin → University → Institution Admin
      → Head Supervisor → Exam Supervisor → Student.
    - Automated email notifications, forced password change on first login,
      intelligent exam creation with subject validation.

WHO ARE THE USERS? (Roles in the System)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. SUPER ADMIN
   - The highest authority (University/Board level).
   - Approves and manages all institutions/colleges on the platform.
   - Can view fraud reports and escalated cases from all institutions.
   - Manages the overall system settings.

2. INSTITUTION ADMIN (College Admin)
   - Manages their specific college's data.
   - Adds students, supervisors, departments, and subjects via CSV upload.
   - When users are added, system automatically sends login credentials
     by email to each student and supervisor.
   - Creates exams — system shows only valid subjects automatically.
   - Manages backlog students separately.
   - Exam is mapped to colleges automatically in one click.

3a. HEAD SUPERVISOR (College Head / Exam Coordinator)
    - Senior authority at the college level.
    - Manages and oversees the entire exam — overall coordination.
    - Assigns Exam Supervisors to specific exam halls.
    - Can view exam status, attendance overview, and all reports.
    - Has authority to escalate fraud/incident cases.

3b. EXAM SUPERVISOR (Hall Incharge)
    - The supervisor physically present inside the exam hall.
    - Scans student QR codes to mark attendance.
    - Verifies student identity by checking details shown after QR scan.
    - Logs fraud/incident reports for that particular hall.

4. STUDENT
   - Added by Institution Admin (not self-registered).
   - Gets email with login credentials automatically.
   - On FIRST LOGIN → system forces student to change their password.
   - Before login → OTP is sent to their email for verification.
   - Can view their exam schedule, admit card, and attendance.

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS - STEP BY STEP (Correct Flow)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: INSTITUTION ONBOARDING (by Super Admin)
    - Super Admin approves a college/institution on the platform.
    - Institution gets a unique Institution Code.
    - Institution Admin account is created.

STEP 2: INSTITUTION SETUP (by Institution Admin)
    - Admin adds:
        → College/Department data
        → Subjects for each course and semester
        → Students (via CSV file upload)
        → Supervisors (Head + Exam supervisors)
    - When students and supervisors are added, the system
      AUTOMATICALLY sends login credentials to their emails.
    - On first login, every user is FORCED to change their password.

STEP 3: OTP BEFORE LOGIN
    - When any user tries to login → OTP is sent to their email.
    - User enters OTP → login is allowed.
    - This ensures only the real person can access the account.

STEP 4: EXAM CREATION (by Institution Admin)
    - Admin creates an exam.
    - System AUTOMATICALLY shows only valid subjects based on:
        → The semester and course
        → If it is a BACKLOG exam → only shows subjects in which
          at least one student has a pending backlog (no extra subjects)
        → If it is a REGULAR exam → shows all subjects for that semester
    - Students are AUTO-MAPPED to the exam based on their course/semester.
    - No manual student list needed — system handles it automatically.
    - Exam is mapped to colleges automatically in one click.

STEP 5: SUPERVISOR HALL ASSIGNMENT (by Head Supervisor)
    - Head Supervisor logs in and assigns Exam Supervisors to halls.
    - NOT done by admin — Head Supervisor manages this independently.

STEP 6: ADMIT CARD GENERATION
    - Once exam is set up, each student gets an Admit Card.
    - Admit Card contains a QR Code (encrypted with AES-256).
    - Student can download or show it on their phone.

STEP 7: EXAM DAY - VERIFICATION PROCESS
    a) Student comes to exam hall and shows their Admit Card (QR Code).
    b) Exam Supervisor (Hall Incharge) opens the Supervisor Dashboard.
    c) Exam Supervisor scans / enters the student's QR Code.
    d) System decrypts the AES-256 encrypted QR and fetches student details.
    e) Exam Supervisor visually confirms the student identity.
    f) Attendance is marked in the system → Record saved ✅
    g) If QR is invalid/tampered → Error shown, Exam Supervisor is alerted ❌
    h) Head Supervisor monitors overall exam and handles escalated cases.

STEP 8: ATTENDANCE & REPORTING
    - All attendance records are saved in the database.
    - Admin can view who attended and who was absent in real-time.
    - Fraud cases are logged with severity (low/medium/high).
    - Super Admin gets escalated fraud alerts.

DATABASE TABLES (For the DBA member to explain)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Our database has 19 tables. Key ones:

TABLE: users
    - Stores ALL users (students, supervisors, admins, super admin).
    - Key fields: user_id, name, email, prn, password, role,
                  supervisor_type (HEAD/EXAM), firstLogin, feesPaid,
                  isEligible, examAccessAllowed, biometric_hash,
                  department, semester, studentType (REGULAR/BACKLOG),
                  college_id, department_id, institution_code

TABLE: institutions
    - Stores each college/institution registered on the platform.
    - Key fields: institution_id, name, institution_code, admin_email,
                  status (pending/approved/rejected), login_key

TABLE: colleges
    - Stores college details linked to an institution.
    - Key fields: college_id, name (linked to institution)

TABLE: departments
    - Stores departments within a college (e.g., Computer, Mechanical).
    - Key fields: department_id, name, college_id

TABLE: subjects
    - Stores subjects for each course, semester, and department.
    - Key fields: id, name, code, semester, course, department_id
    - Unique constraint: code + semester + department + course

TABLE: exams
    - Stores all exam details.
    - Key fields: exam_id, exam_name, subject_id, type (REGULAR/BACKLOG),
                  semester, college_id, date, start_time, duration,
                  mode (online/offline), status (upcoming/completed),
                  supervisor_id, supervisor_ids

TABLE: qr_codes
    - Stores the encrypted QR tokens for each student-exam pair.
    - Key fields: qr_id, user_id, exam_id, token (AES-256 encrypted)

TABLE: attendance
    - Records when a student's attendance was marked by supervisor.
    - Key fields: id, exam_id, student_id, supervisor_id, marked_on

TABLE: exam_attendance
    - Extended attendance with more details.
    - Key fields: exam_id, student_id, supervisor_id, status

TABLE: student_backlogs
    - Tracks backlog subjects for each student.
    - Key fields: id, student_id, subject_id, subject_name,
                  semester, cleared (true/false)

TABLE: fraud_logs
    - Logs all suspicious activity during exams.
    - Key fields: log_id, user_id, exam_id, description,
                  severity (low/medium/high), escalated_to_super_admin

TABLE: otp_verifications
    - Stores OTPs sent to users for email login verification.
    - Key fields: id, email, otp_code, created_at, expires_at, verified

TABLE: communication_messages
    - Stores messages/notifications sent between roles.
    - Key fields: id, senderEmail, receiverEmail, senderRole, receiverRole,
                  subject, message, priority (LOW/NORMAL/URGENT),
                  type (GENERAL/WARNING/BROADCAST), status (SENT/READ)

TABLE: incident_reports
    - Logs incidents reported by Exam Supervisor during exam.
    - Key fields: report_id, supervisor_id, exam_id, description

TABLE: supervisor_action_logs
    - Logs all actions taken by supervisors (audit trail).
    - Key fields: id, supervisor_id, action, timestamp

TABLE: system_settings
    - Stores global system configuration settings.
    - Key fields: setting_id, key, value

TABLE: alerts
    - Stores alerts sent within the system.
    - Key fields: alert_id, user_id, message, type, created_at

TABLE: qr_code_entries
    - Detailed QR code scan log.
    - Key fields: entry_id, qr_id, scanned_by, scanned_at

TABLE: documents
    - Stores uploaded documents/files for user profiles.
    - Key fields: doc_id, user_id, doc_type, doc_path,
                  verified_status, uploaded_on

AI MODULE (Python Flask - Port 5001)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Our system has a Python Flask microservice (app.py) on Port 5001.
Currently it is used for BIOMETRIC verification support only.

HOW BIOMETRIC WORKS (Java - BiometricController.java):
    1. Exam Supervisor captures the student's fingerprint template.
    2. Java backend hashes the fingerprint using SHA-256.
    3. If student has NO stored hash → ENROLLS them (first scan).
    4. If student HAS a stored hash → COMPARES both hashes.
    5. If hashes MATCH → Attendance marked as PRESENT ✔️
    6. If hashes DON'T MATCH → Attendance marked as ABSENT / FAILED ❌

NOTE: Face verification and Document OCR are written in Python
      but are NOT active in the current demo. These are planned
      for future implementation (see Future Scope section).

CHALLENGES WE FACED (Say this confidently!)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. AES-256 QR Code Encryption/Decryption
   - Implementing AES-256 encryption for QR tokens was complex.
   - Had to ensure both generation (admin side) and decryption
     (supervisor scan side) worked perfectly without data loss.

2. Multi-Role Access Control (5 Roles)
   - Had to carefully restrict which user can access which API.
   - 5 different roles: Super Admin, Institution Admin, Head Supervisor,
     Exam Supervisor, Student — each with different permissions.
   - Used JWT tokens with role-based authorization.

3. Intelligent Exam Subject Validation
   - System had to automatically filter subjects based on exam type.
   - For backlog exam: only show subjects where at least one student
     has a pending backlog — this required complex query logic.

4. Dual Supervisor Role Design
   - Head Supervisor (College Head) manages the overall exam.
   - Exam Supervisor (Hall Incharge) handles on-ground verification.
   - Designing separate access levels and responsibilities required
     careful planning of the system architecture.

5. Automated Email on User Creation
   - When admin adds students/supervisors via CSV, the system
     automatically sends email to each user with credentials.
   - Handling bulk email for hundreds of users efficiently was tricky.

6. Forced First Login Password Change
   - When a user logs in for the first time, system must detect it
     and force them to set a new password before accessing anything.
   - Used a firstLogin flag in the users table to handle this.

7. CSV Bulk Upload Parsing
   - Parsing CSV files and inserting hundreds of student/supervisor
     records without errors or duplicates was challenging.

DISADVANTAGES / CURRENT LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

    ❌ 1. INTERNET REQUIRED
          The system needs active internet to work.
          If the network goes down during exam, QR scanning stops.

    ❌ 2. REAL BIOMETRIC NOT INTEGRATED
          We could not integrate real fingerprint scanner hardware
          because real biometric systems require a private paid domain
          and specialized vendor SDK/network access — not available
          in an academic project setup. Currently we use biometric
          template matching which simulates the same logic and can
          be connected to real hardware in the future.

    ❌ 3. NO MOBILE APP
          Currently only a web application.
          Students must access it from a browser (laptop/desktop).

    ❌ 4. NO OFFLINE MODE
          If the server goes down, supervisors cannot mark attendance
          until it comes back online.

QUICK ANSWERS TO COMMON EVALUATOR QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Why did you use Spring Boot?
A: Spring Boot is industry-standard for building secure, scalable REST APIs
   in Java. It has built-in security, database connectivity, and is
   widely used in real-world enterprise applications.

Q: How does biometric/fingerprint verification work?
A: It is handled entirely in our Java Spring Boot backend.
   When the supervisor scans a student's fingerprint, Java hashes
   it using SHA-256 and compares it with the stored hash in the
   database. If it is the first scan, it enrolls the student.
   If the hashes match, attendance is marked as PRESENT.
   If not, it is marked as FAILED/ABSENT.

Q: How secure is the QR code?
A: The QR code is encrypted using AES-256 (military-grade encryption).
   Even if someone takes a screenshot of the QR, they cannot forge it.

Q: Why didn't you use real fingerprint scanner hardware?
A: Real biometric fingerprint scanners require a private paid domain
   and specialized network/SDK integration provided by hardware vendors.
   These are paid services and not accessible in an academic project
   environment. So we implemented biometric template matching which
   simulates the same logic and can be connected to real hardware later.

Q: What is the difference between Head Supervisor and Exam Supervisor?
A: Head Supervisor is the college head / exam coordinator — manages
   the entire exam, assigns Exam Supervisors to halls, and handles
   escalated fraud/incident cases at a higher authority level.
   Exam Supervisor is the hall incharge — physically present inside
   the exam hall, scans student QR codes, marks attendance, and
   reports incidents directly in the system.

Q: How does smart subject selection work in exam creation?
A: When admin creates an exam, they choose the type — REGULAR or BACKLOG.
   For REGULAR: system shows all subjects for that semester/course.
   For BACKLOG: system only shows subjects where at least one student
   has a pending backlog. This prevents selecting irrelevant subjects.

Q: How are students notified about exams?
A: The system sends automated emails. When admin adds students via CSV,
   credentials are emailed automatically. Students are also forced to
   change their password on first login for security.

Q: Who assigns supervisors to exam halls?
A: The Head Supervisor does this — NOT the institution admin.
   Head Supervisor logs in and assigns Exam Supervisors to halls.

Q: How does the system handle multiple colleges?
A: The Super Admin can onboard multiple institutions. Each institution
   has its own admin, students, supervisors, and exam data. Data is
   completely isolated per institution.

Q: What if the internet goes down during the exam?
A: This is a known limitation. As future scope, we plan to add an
   offline mode where basic QR validation works without internet.
