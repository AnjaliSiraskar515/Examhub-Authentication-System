                       OVERVIEW — TWO TYPES OF SUPERVISORS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

In our ExamHub system, there are TWO types of supervisors.
Both login using the same page (supervisor_dashboard.html), but they see
different things and do different jobs.

  Type 1 → HEAD SUPERVISOR   (manages the college side of things)
  Type 2 → EXAM SUPERVISOR   (manages the exam hall on the day of exam)

Think of it like this:
  - Head Supervisor = College Manager / Administrative Head
  - Exam Supervisor = Exam Hall Invigilator


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   PART 1 — HEAD SUPERVISOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHO IS THE HEAD SUPERVISOR?
───────────────────────────
The Head Supervisor is a senior person assigned to one specific college.
For example: Head Supervisor for SRCOE college.

They are responsible for the MANAGEMENT side — not for sitting in exam halls.
They work BEFORE and AFTER the exam, not during it.


WHAT DOES THE HEAD SUPERVISOR DO?
──────────────────────────────────

1. EXAM FORM APPROVALS
   - When a student submits their exam form (registration), the Head Supervisor
     reviews it.
   - They check if the student's documents are correct, fees are paid, and
     eligibility is met.
   - They can APPROVE or REJECT the exam form.
   - After approval, the student gets their admit card and QR code.

2. COLLEGE EXAM OVERVIEW
   - They can see ALL exams scheduled for their college.
   - They see the full schedule: exam name, date, time, mode (online/offline),
     exam center, and how many students are registered.
   - This is like a college-wide exam timetable visible to them.

3. MONITORING THE STRUCTURE
   - They see summary stats:
       → Total number of exams in their college
       → Total students registered across all exams
       → How many exams are currently LIVE or UPCOMING

4. UNIVERSITY COORDINATION
   - If there are any issues with exams (wrong center, schedule clash, etc.),
     the Head Supervisor is the point of contact between the college and
     the university.


WHAT HEAD SUPERVISOR DOES NOT DO:
──────────────────────────────────
  ✗ Does NOT scan QR codes on exam day
  ✗ Does NOT do biometric (fingerprint) verification of students
  ✗ Does NOT sit in the exam hall
  ✗ Does NOT supervise the students in real time during the exam


WHICH COLLEGE ARE THEY ASSIGNED TO?
────────────────────────────────────
  - Each Head Supervisor is tied to ONE specific college (e.g., SRCOE).
  - They can ONLY see exams and students from that college.
  - This is controlled by the `college_id` field in the database.


TECHNICAL DETAILS (Head Supervisor):
──────────────────────────────────────
  - Database field: users.supervisor_type = 'HEAD'
  - College restriction: YES — filtered by college_id in the backend
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/exam-forms (Exam Form Approvals API)
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/exams (College exam list)
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/profile (Their profile and college info)
  - CANNOT access QR scan flow
  - Dashboard UI: Shows "Exams Overview" tab (not "Exams Assigned")
  - Dashboard UI: 4 stat blocks (QR Scans, Pending Verifications etc.) are HIDDEN
  - Dashboard UI: Shows professional college-wide exam schedule table
  - Sidebar shows: My Profile | Exams Overview | Exam Form Approvals | Logout
  - Role badge shown in profile: 👑 Head Supervisor

EXAMPLE (in our project):
  - Name: Supervisor B
  - Email: examhub001@gmail.com
  - College: SRCOE
  - supervisor_type: HEAD

   PART 2 — EXAM SUPERVISOR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WHO IS THE EXAM SUPERVISOR?
────────────────────────────
The Exam Supervisor is the person who actually RUNS the exam on the day.
They are assigned to specific exams by the university admin.
They can be assigned to exams at ANY college — not restricted to one college.

Think of them as the invigilator who sits in the exam hall.


WHAT DOES THE EXAM SUPERVISOR DO?
───────────────────────────────────

1. SCANNING QR CODE
   - When a student enters the exam hall, the Exam Supervisor scans their
     QR code using the camera on the device.
   - The QR code is on the student's admit card.
   - After scanning, the student's details appear on screen (name, PRN,
     photo, subjects, etc.)
   - The student is marked as QR-verified in the database.

2. BIOMETRIC VERIFICATION
   - After QR scan, the Exam Supervisor does FINGERPRINT (biometric) verification.
   - The student places their finger on the scanner.
   - Our system is a SIMULATOR — it simulates the biometric process:
       Step 1: "Acquiring fingerprint..." (scanning animation)
       Step 2: "Matching print..." (comparison animation)
       Step 3: FIRST ATTEMPT → Shows "PARTIAL PRINT DETECTED" (mismatch - red)
       Step 4: "RE-INITIALIZING SCANNER" (retry - yellow)
       Step 5: SECOND ATTEMPT → "VERIFIED ✓" (green - success)
   - This retry simulation makes the process feel realistic.
   - Once verified, student's biometric_verified = true in the database.

3. LIVE MONITORING
   - Exam Supervisor sees a LIVE table of all students in their exam:
       → Student name, PRN, Roll No.
       → Verification status (QR verified, Biometric verified, or Pending)
       → Live status (APPROVED, PENDING)
       → Activity timestamps
       → Warning flags if student is idle too long
   - They can report incidents (cheating, disturbance, etc.)

4. EXAM CONTROLS
   - They can PAUSE the exam (if there's an issue)
   - They can trigger EMERGENCY LOCK (completely locks the exam)
   - They can END the exam when time is up
   - They can BROADCAST announcements to all students

5. ATTENDANCE SHEET
   - After the exam, they can download a PDF attendance sheet
     showing which students were verified and present.


WHAT EXAM SUPERVISOR DOES NOT DO:
───────────────────────────────────
  ✗ Does NOT approve exam forms (that's the Head Supervisor's job)
  ✗ Does NOT have restriction to one college — can supervise any college's exam


WHICH COLLEGE ARE THEY ASSIGNED TO?
────────────────────────────────────
  - EXAM supervisors have NO college restriction.
  - They are assigned to specific EXAMS (not colleges).
  - The university admin assigns them directly to exams.
  - They can be sent to SRCOE, Dhole Patil, GH Raisoni, or any college.


TECHNICAL DETAILS (Exam Supervisor):
──────────────────────────────────────
  - Database field: users.supervisor_type = 'EXAM'
  - College restriction: NO — no college filter applied in backend
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/exams (Only exams assigned to them via supervisor_id)
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/students?examId=X (Students for specific exam)
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/verify-qr (QR code verification endpoint)
  - Can access: /api/supervisor/mark-attendance (Mark student present)
  - Dashboard UI: Shows 4 stat blocks (Exams Today, Students Assigned, QR Scans, Pending)
  - Dashboard UI: Shows "Exams Assigned" tab listing all their assigned exams
  - Dashboard UI: Has QR scanner, Live Monitoring table, Biometric modal
  - Sidebar shows: My Profile | Exams Assigned | Logout
  - Role badge shown in profile: Exam Supervisor
  - Biometric: Uses deterministicHash(studentId) simulation for fingerprint matching

EXAMPLE (in our project):
  - Name: Supervisor A
  - Email: photosfor544@gmail.com
  - College: SRCOE (but NOT restricted by it)
  - supervisor_type: EXAM

   PART 3 — STUDENT VERIFICATION PROCESS (WHO DOES WHAT)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BEFORE EXAM DAY:
────────────────
  Student submits exam form
        ↓
  HEAD SUPERVISOR reviews the form
        ↓
  HEAD SUPERVISOR approves / rejects
        ↓
  If approved → Student gets Admit Card with QR Code

ON EXAM DAY:
────────────
  Student arrives at exam hall
        ↓
  EXAM SUPERVISOR scans QR code on student's admit card
        ↓
  Student details appear on screen
        ↓
  EXAM SUPERVISOR initiates biometric verification
        ↓
  Student places finger on scanner
        ↓
  System simulates: Partial print → Retry → Verified ✓
        ↓
  Student's status = VERIFIED (both QR + Biometric)
        ↓
  Student is allowed to sit for exam

   PART 5 — HOW BIOMETRIC WORKS IN OUR SYSTEM (Technical)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Our biometric is a SIMULATOR (not a real hardware device).
This is because in a project environment, we cannot attach real fingerprint
scanners to every device. Instead, we simulate the entire process:

HOW IT WORKS:
  1. Supervisor clicks "Scan QR" → student details load
  2. Supervisor clicks "Verify Biometric" button
  3. A modal opens with an animated fingerprint scanner UI
  4. The system follows this sequence:

     Phase 1 — ACQUIRING (2 seconds)
       → Shows animated fingerprint scanning
       → Text: "Acquiring fingerprint data..."

     Phase 2 — MATCHING (1.5 seconds)
       → Text: "Matching against enrollment database..."
       → Shows matching percentage climbing

     Phase 3 — MISMATCH (1 second) ← FORCED FIRST ATTEMPT FAILURE
       → Red animation
       → Text: "PARTIAL PRINT DETECTED — Match Score: 61%"
       → This failure is INTENTIONAL to make it look realistic

     Phase 4 — RE-INITIALIZING (1.5 seconds)
       → Yellow/amber color
       → Text: "RE-INITIALIZING SCANNER — Please re-place finger"

     Phase 5 — SECOND SCAN (1 second)
       → Text: "Acquiring second sample..."

     Phase 6 — VERIFIED (success)
       → Green animation
       → Text: "IDENTITY VERIFIED — Match: 98.7%"
       → Student's biometric_verified = true stored in database

WHY FORCE A MISMATCH FIRST?
  - Real fingerprint scanners often fail on first attempt due to placement.
  - This makes our simulator feel REALISTIC for demo and viva purposes.
  - It shows the system handles errors gracefully and retries.

DETERMINISTIC HASH:
  - Each student has a unique simulated fingerprint hash.
  - It is generated as: "PHYSICAL_MINUTIAE_" + hash(studentId)
  - This means the same student always produces the same hash.
  - So the system is consistent across sessions.

REAL HARDWARE FUTURE:
  - If real fingerprint hardware is connected later, only the
    verifyIdentityForScan() function needs to be replaced.
  - The rest of the UI, backend, and database remain unchanged.

   PART 7 — COMMON QUESTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Can the Head Supervisor also supervise exams?
A: No. Their role is purely administrative. They don't have the QR scanner
   or biometric tools on their dashboard.

Q: Can the Exam Supervisor approve exam forms?
A: No. The "Exam Form Approvals" menu item is hidden for Exam Supervisors.
   Only Head Supervisors can see and use it.

Q: Who assigns the Exam Supervisor to an exam?
A: The University Admin assigns them when creating an exam using the
   university exam wizard (university admin dashboard).

Q: Who creates the Head Supervisor account?
A: The University Admin creates all supervisor accounts and sets the
   supervisor_type to HEAD or EXAM.

Q: Can a supervisor be of both types?
A: No. One account = one type. If someone needs both roles, two separate
   accounts must be created.

Q: If a student has no college_id set, can the Exam Supervisor still see them?
A: Yes! Exam Supervisors bypass the college filter. They see all students
   registered for their assigned exams regardless of college_id on the student.

Q: What happens if the biometric scan fails permanently?
A: Currently the simulator always succeeds on second attempt. In a real system,
   3 failed attempts could trigger a manual verification by the Head Supervisor.
